# Walmart course fallback report

## Overview

The saved annual filing describes operations across retail, wholesale, other units, and eCommerce in the United States, Africa, Canada, Central America, Chile, China, India, and Mexico. [10-K.txt:L147] The filing identifies three reportable segments: Walmart U.S., Walmart International, and Sam's Club U.S. [10-K.txt:L140-L147]

The annual income statement reports total revenues of $713,163 million for fiscal 2026, made up of net sales of $706,413 million and membership and other income of $6,750 million. [10-K.txt:L919-L925] It reports operating income of $29,825 million and consolidated net income attributable to Walmart of $21,893 million. [10-K.txt:L925-L937]

**Insight — Research Analyst:** The saved filing identifies three reportable segments, so consolidated performance reflects more than one retail format and geography. [10-K.txt:L140-L147]

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

The filing states that the fiscal 2026 total-revenues figure primarily comprised net sales of $706.4 billion, while total revenues were $713.2 billion. [10-K.txt:L140] This distinction matters because net sales alone is narrower than total revenues.

The saved quarterly release reports total revenues of $187,937 million for the three months ended July 31, 2026, compared with $177,402 million for the prior-year three-month period. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L162-L168] It reports quarterly operating income of $9,383 million, compared with $7,286 million. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L168-L172]

The release reports quarterly diluted net income per common share attributable to Walmart of $0.80, compared with $0.88 in the prior-year quarter. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L187-L189] These are quarterly GAAP results; the separate adjusted measures in the release are not substituted for them.

## Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

The filing says that pricing and merchandising strategies respond to cost increases and can affect gross profit as a percentage of net sales. [10-K.txt:L506] It describes margin improvement through productivity initiatives and category and business mix, including higher-margin digital advertising. [10-K.txt:L512-L530]

The filing also identifies risks from consumer demand, currency exchange rates, tariffs, trade restrictions, and merchandise mix that could adversely affect net sales and operating income. [10-K.txt:L275] These are disclosed risks, not forecasts.
