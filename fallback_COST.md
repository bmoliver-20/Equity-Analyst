# COST course fallback report

## Overview

The saved annual filing describes a membership-warehouse and e-commerce model that seeks high sales volume and rapid inventory turnover through low prices, limited selection, efficient distribution, and self-service facilities. [10-K.txt:L97]

The annual income statement reports total revenue of $303,154 million for the fiscal year ended August 30, 2026, including net sales of $297,247 million and membership fees of $5,907 million. [10-K.txt:L596-L600] It reports operating income of $11,685 million and net income of $9,226 million. [10-K.txt:L604-L612]

**Insight — Research Analyst:** The stated model depends on low prices, high volume, and rapid inventory turnover, rather than a claim of high gross margins. [10-K.txt:L97]

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

The annual filing states that modest changes in merchandise costs and selling, general, and administrative expense can materially affect net income because the business operates on low margins. [10-K.txt:L379]

The latest earnings release reports fourth-quarter total revenue of $95,723 million, compared with $86,156 million in the prior-year quarter. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L43-L49] Fourth-quarter operating income was $3,801 million, compared with $3,341 million in the prior-year quarter. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L49-L53]

The release reports fourth-quarter diluted EPS of $6.75, compared with $5.87 in the prior-year quarter. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L63-L65] It also says the fourth quarter included a non-recurring benefit of $0.15 per diluted share from tariff refunds, less partial reinvestment in member value. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L25]

## Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

The annual filing identifies cost control as important to financial performance and notes that health-care and utility costs are partly outside management’s control. [10-K.txt:L379] It also identifies risks from slowing comparable-warehouse sales, higher operating costs, missed warehouse-opening targets, cannibalization, lower-margin sales mix, and economic conditions. [10-K.txt:L186]

The release reports that the company operated 939 warehouses at the reported date. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L26] This is a reported operating fact, not a forecast of future openings or sales.

## Investment Recommendation

**Hold — provisional educational judgment.** The saved statements show positive operating cash generation and annual total revenue, while the release identifies a non-recurring fourth-quarter EPS benefit and the sources provide no share price. [10-K.txt:L596-L600] [10-K.txt:L714-L733] [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L25]

The annual income statement reports diluted EPS of $20.76 for the fiscal year ended August 30, 2026. [10-K.txt:L613-L615] The quarterly release reports $6.75 diluted EPS for the fourth quarter and $20.76 for the fiscal year; these are distinct periods and should not be substituted for one another. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L63-L65]

**Insight — Investment Advisor:** The fourth-quarter diluted EPS of $6.75 includes a disclosed non-recurring benefit, so it should not by itself support a recurring-earnings conclusion. [earnings_release_99_1.txt:L25]
